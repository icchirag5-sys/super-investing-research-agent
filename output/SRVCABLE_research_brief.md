# SRVCABLE: Research Brief (Sarvottam Cables Ltd)
*Reference date: 23 September 2026. Not investment advice. Built only from the supplied research pack.*

## 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires and conductors [S2.1]. In its latest reported results for Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30 June 2026) [S2.1], revenue from operations rose 18.0% year-on-year to ₹1,248 crore, EBITDA reached ₹140 crore (up 2.6%), and profit after tax was ₹82 crore (up 5.9%) [S2.2]. The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026 [S2.3].

## 2. Bull case
- *Reported:* Healthy order intake and expanding exports: The order book grew to ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026 from ₹3,150 crore a year ago [S2.3], while exports rose to 21% of revenue compared to 16% in Q1 FY26 [S2.4].
- *Reported:* Capacity expansion on track with moderate leverage: The new Bharuch plant is scheduled to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27, adding about 30% to cable capacity [S2.5]. About 70 percent of the ₹620 crore capex is already spent, while net debt to equity stands at 0.35 times [S1.7].
- *Reported:* Significant reduction in promoter pledge: Pledged shares as a percentage of promoter holding dropped steadily from 11.5% in June 2025 to 4.1% in June 2026 [S7.1].
- *Management says:* Full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance is maintained at 15 to 17 percent, driven by strong demand in power transmission and data centres [S1.1][S2.6]. Data-centre cables account for about 9 percent of domestic revenue, which management believes can double in two years [S1.1].

## 3. Bear case
- *Reported:* Margin compression from raw materials: EBITDA margin dropped 170 basis points year-on-year to 11.2% in Q1 FY27 [S2.2], primarily due to higher copper prices [S1.3][S2.6]. While two-thirds of contracts contain price-variation clauses, price adjustments lag by one to two quarters [S1.3].
- *Reported:* Working capital stretch: Receivable days (the time customers take to pay) rose from 78 to 96 over the past year, mainly due to slower payments from two large state utility customers [S1.5].
- *Reported:* Substantial tax demand: The company received a CGST demand order of ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) regarding input tax credit claims for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [S6.1].
- *Our read:* The ₹46.3 crore tax demand is material, amounting to more than half of Q1 FY27 profit after tax of ₹82 crore (derived: 46.3 ÷ 82 = 56.5%) [S2.2][S6.1]. Management's assertion that it expects no material impact is an unproven claim pending appeal [S6.2].

## 4. Open questions
- *Tax dispute outcome:* Will the planned appeal against the ₹46.3 crore GST order succeed, or will the dispute result in cash outflows or provisions [S6.1][S6.2]?
- *Working capital recovery:* Can management collect pending dues from the two state utility customers and reduce receivable days without bad debt write-offs [S1.5]?
- *Execution timeline:* Will the Bharuch facility commence commercial production in Q3 FY27 without cost overruns or commissioning delays [S1.7][S2.5]?
- *Disregarded and conflicting sources:* Source S5 was excluded because it reports an ₹8 lakh penalty on "Sarvottam Cable Network", an unrelated local cable TV and broadband firm [S5.1]. Source S4 was rejected as an unverified promotional blog claiming false 35% revenue growth (conflicting with reported 18.0% [S2.2]), a ₹1,450 price target, and quarantined prompt-injection instructions [S4.1][S4.4]. A March 2024 news article reporting a 35% promoter pledge [S3.1] is outdated; regulatory filings confirm the pledge has fallen to 4.1% as of June 2026 [S7.1].
